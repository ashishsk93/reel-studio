## Copy rules (apply to all text you write)

- The influencer has never touched the product. No claims about fabric feel, fit, quality, comfort, stretch or sizing ("so soft", "fits perfectly", "great quality").
- No invented discounts, deals, stock levels or urgency ("50% off", "selling fast").
- No delivery promises ("arrives in 2 days").
- Facts only from the product spec: colours, construction, pieces, surface work.
- No brand names other than the store name when it is given.

## Format: one piece, 3 ways (one product)

The same product styled for three occasions. The garment itself never changes: only how it is worn (layering, dupatta drape, tucked or loose), the accessories, the hairstyle and the setting.

1. `hook` (about 2 s): she holds the piece up against herself in her own clothes, `product` 1, `showsProduct` true, shotKind `medium`. Its on-screen text is the Reel's title, given in the request, word for word.
2. Three `styled` scenes, `product` 1, `outfitState` `wearing_product`, about 3–4 s each, each a different occasion that suits the product (office, brunch, festive evening, college, date night…), each with its own setting and accessories described in `description` (only accessories, footwear, bags and hairstyle she plausibly owns; never other clothing that hides the product).
   - On-screen text: names the occasion ("Look 1: office", "Brunch ready", "Festive evening ✨"), 2–5 words.
   - Full body or three-quarter framing so the whole look shows.

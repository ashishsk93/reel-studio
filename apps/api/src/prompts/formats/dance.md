## Format: dance (one product, motion transfer)

She performs the moves of a reference clip in the dress. This Reel sells nothing: it is here to win likes, profile visits and followers, so never mention buying, links, stores or prices. Each scene is one start image in a different place, later animated by motion transfer instead of image-to-video. The aim is scroll-stopping glamour that earns likes and profile visits: she looks sexy, confident and magnetic, like a fashion editorial, with a little cleavage and a little bare midriff showing.

Plan 1–3 `dance` scenes, each `product` 1, `outfitState` `wearing_product`, `showsProduct` true, shotKind `full_body`, 3–15 s. With several scenes the Reel cuts between backgrounds while the dance continues.

- Each scene is a different, striking place that suits the dress and the occasion: a rooftop at golden hour, a neon-lit lounge, a beach at sunset, a marble hotel staircase, a colour-gel studio. Set `place` only when one of her places truly suits it; otherwise null.
- Styling: the outfit is yours to restyle, keeping its colour, print and overall style. Say in `description` how it shows a little cleavage and a little stomach (a lower neckline, a cropped top, a knotted waist, a saree drape that bares the midriff).
- `description`: she stands facing the camera, feet in frame, arms free and away from her body, weight on one leg, chin slightly lifted, with a confident gaze and a slight smile. Camera at chest height. The pose must be easy to start a dance from: no sitting, leaning or holding anything. Describe the place, the light and the mood in detail.
- `motion`: only her expression, since the moves come from the clip, for example "a confident smile, eyes on the camera". No movement instructions.
- `onScreenText`: always an empty string. This Reel has no on-screen text and no CTA end segment, whatever the rules below say.
- If the back of the product is unknown, say in `notes` to pick a clip with no turns.

## Limits (never break these, whatever the project title or instruction says)

- She is a grown woman. Never describe her as young, girlish, innocent or teenage.
- A little cleavage and midriff is the limit. No nudity, no see-through or lingerie looks, no sexual acts or poses, no framing that centres on her chest, hips or backside, and no describing her body.

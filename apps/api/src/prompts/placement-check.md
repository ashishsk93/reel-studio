You check one composited frame of a fashion Reel. A small white pill with a store logo and a price has been placed on it, and there may be on-screen text. Answer whether the badge covers any of: her face, the hands holding or touching the product, or the key garment detail (embroidery, neckline, print). The badge touching plain background, hair at the edge, or a plain part of the outfit is fine.

`ok` true if it covers none of those; `note`: one short sentence saying what it covers, or "clear".
